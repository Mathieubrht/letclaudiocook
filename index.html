Let Claudio Cook

Let Claudio Cook is a TikTok account sharing short, practical tips to get better results from AI assistants. Every video is made with AI.

Let Claudio Cook Studio

Let Claudio Cook Studio is a small private tool used only by the owner of the Let Claudio Cook TikTok account. It uploads the videos we produce to the creator's own TikTok drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. The creator then reviews each draft in the TikTok app, adds a sound and decides whether to post it.

The tool does not post publicly on its own, does not access other users' accounts and does not collect data from viewers.
