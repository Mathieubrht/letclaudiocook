Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data Let Claudio Cook Studio (the "Service") handles. The Service is a private tool used only by the owner of the Let Claudio Cook TikTok account.

1. Data we access

When the creator authorizes the Service through TikTok's official login, TikTok provides:

  • basic account information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected;
  • an access token allowing the Service to upload videos to that account's drafts (video.upload).

2. How it is stored

Tokens and account information are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a local file that is never shared or published. No database, analytics or tracking is used. This website itself sets no cookies and collects no data.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to perform the upload the creator requested.

4. Viewers

The Service does not access, collect or process any data about people who watch the account's videos.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the Service is in use. The creator can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions); the local token file is then deleted.

6. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Mathieubrht/letclaudiocook/issues.
